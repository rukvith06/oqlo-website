Who We Are

Pioneering Autonomous Spatial Data Intelligence

OQLO combines high-precision RTK/PPK drone hardware, cloud photogrammetry, and spatial artificial intelligence to turn complex site topography into crystal-clear digital models. We empower engineers, survey teams, and project owners to make fast, informed decisions with sub-5 cm accuracy.

48-Hour Delivery Commitment

From initial flight takeoff to fully processed CAD deliverables and 3D web models in under 48 hours.

Centimeter Precision Standard

RTK/PPK geo-tagging guarantees pinpoint ~99% accuracy exceeding traditional land surveys.
